          <p class="pg-callout-body">
            Trimester 1 was about getting my footing. We set up the tools that would carry
            us through the entire year &mdash; GitHub, VS Code, and the command line &mdash; and
            dove into the fundamentals of Python and JavaScript. Collaborative group projects
            introduced me to the pace of real development: my team was sharp and ambitious,
            which pushed me to level up quickly. Grasping asynchronous programming was an
            early challenge, but working through the confusion taught me more than getting it
            right on the first try ever would have.
